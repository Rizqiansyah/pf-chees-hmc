# WP1 2×2 — metric ordering × metric orientation

Why this file exists: WP1 changed two things at once (the tuple→dict **ordering** of the metric, and a
claim about its **orientation**), and a single-arm before/after comparison cannot attribute anything.
This is the four-cell decomposition, run in **one tree with one key stream** so the Pathfinder fit,
the starts and every RNG draw are identical and only the two factors differ
(`imm_order="pathfinder"` skips the reorder; `imm_orientation` selects which side of the covariance is
handed to the kernel). Fixture A is the deciding one; fixture B is kept because it is the fixture
Part B specified and because it shows how this experiment can be designed so that it proves nothing.

Reproduce: `tools/summarise_rows.py` for the R2 arms; the 2×2 scripts are `~/scratch/wp1_2x2b.py`
(fixture A, CPU) and `~/scratch/wp1_2x2.py` (fixture B, CPU), commands in SUMMARY §8. Cell ids below
are `(model, order, orientation, seed)`.

Definitions used throughout: A = the array passed as `inverse_mass_matrix`. The wheel computes
`KE = 1/2 p·(A p)` and momentum `~ N(0, A⁻¹)` (`mcmc/metrics.py:262-270`, verified by measurement in
`test_kernel_convention_is_ke_half_p_A_p_and_momentum_cov_is_A_inverse`), so `q'' = -A·∇U` and the
squared frequencies of a quadratic target with Hessian H are `eig(A·H)`. `orientation="covariance"`
⇒ A = Cov ⇒ all frequencies 1; `orientation="precision"` ⇒ A = Cov⁻¹ ⇒ frequency 1/var per
coordinate, which a single global ε cannot serve. A has a **second consumer**: the ChEES
trajectory-length whitening uses `jnp.diag(imm)` (`pfcheeshmc/utils.py`, fed to `update_adapt`), so an
ordering or orientation error also mis-whitens the adapter, not just the kernel.

Only one cell is well-conditioned: **permuted + covariance**. The two `precision` cells fail at
R̂ 10–13 with min bulk ESS 32–38 and worst-variable mean error 0.19–0.30 posterior sd, and the failure
has the orientation signature — every wide coordinate dies at once (alpha, delta, zeta all ~33) while
the tight one (beta, var 1e-2) keeps ~33,000, exactly as `frequency = 1/var` predicts.

**The deciding cell is `kernel + precision`** (ordering correct, only orientation wrong): R̂ 12.10–13.08,
min bulk ESS 32–38, against R̂ 1.0020–1.0022 and ESS 30,121–33,495 for `kernel + covariance`. So the
orientation is not a tuning preference: at a normal budget it misses the project's G-A bar (R̂ ≤ 1.01)
by an order of magnitude and loses ~1000× worst-variable ESS per gradient.

The `pathfinder + covariance` cell isolates **ordering**: mean error stays small (0.013–0.020 sd), two
of three seeds read R̂ ≈ 1.007, but exactly one coordinate collapses — zeta (variance 1e2), ESS 166–3,176
against ~26,000 for the others — and on seed 6 the whole run reads R̂ 1.1451. Ordering errors misplace
specific coordinates; orientation errors flatten the whole wide half of the space. Both are silent.

## Fixture B — DEGENERATE, kept as a design warning

Three scalars, variances 1e1 / 1e0 / 1e-2, created reverse-sorted (`[zeta, beta, alpha]` vs kernel
`[alpha, beta, zeta]`), the fixture Part B §WP1 implied. The permutation is a reversal and the variance
ladder is symmetric about 1, so "read an unpermuted covariance" ≈ "read a permuted precision": **two

## Real-model cells (GEV Port Pirie), where `value_vars` order ≠ kernel order

GEV is `[mu, sigma_log__, xi_interval__]` vs kernel `[mu, xi_interval__, sigma_log__]` — the orders
actually differ and the swapped coordinates have different scales, so this is the ladder model on which
the same four cells must be re-run (`results/runs/WP1__gev_2x2.jsonl`, 32 chains, 1,250 draws, 2,000
warmup, seeds 43/44/45, warm rows, one tree).

Partial evidence already in the R2 matrix (`results/runs/WP1__rebaseline.jsonl`, warm, 5 seeds), which
is why the real-model 2×2 was added:

| GEV arm | ε | L_nom | steps_mean | max R̂ | min bulk ESS | ESS/s worst | grads/ESS |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| `legacy:mc` = pathfinder + precision | 0.0009 | 54 | 27.0 | 1.0075 | 8,102 | 403 | 73.4 |
| `wp1:mc` = kernel + precision (shipped default) | 0.0009 | 8 | 4.1 | **1.0688** | **679** | **44** | **295.8** |

Fixing the ordering **while leaving the orientation at `precision` makes GEV worse**, which is the
interaction fixture A predicts: on GEV the legacy pair of errors partially cancels. The two
covariance-orientation cells on GEV (kernel + covariance, pathfinder + covariance) are what settle it,
and they are in flight.
